## Reusable experiment template

Copy the following fields into a new record when starting work:

- **Name and status:** planned, ongoing, paused, or completed.
- **Date:** when the work actually took place; label approximate or retrospective dates.
- **Objective:** the question being investigated.
- **Hardware and software:** relevant models and versions.
- **Starting state:** configuration and assumptions before the change.
- **Hypothesis:** expected behavior and reasoning.
- **Steps:** actions performed, in order.
- **Results:** observed behavior and relevant evidence.
- **Problems:** symptoms, possible causes, and remaining uncertainty.
- **What I learned:** conclusions supported by the observations.
- **Next steps:** the next change or question.
- **Rollback/cleanup:** how to return to the starting state.
